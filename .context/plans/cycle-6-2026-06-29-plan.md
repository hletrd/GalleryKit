## Work Items

### 1. Restore lifecycle hardening

Status: DONE  
Findings: C6-01, C6-02, C6-03, C6-34

Implementation:
- Make restore lock acquisition cleanup-owned from the first acquired lock through transfer into the restore window.
- Keep advisory/upload locks releasable even if setup queries throw.
- Only end restore maintenance and resume the image queue after import and post-restore migrations succeed.
- Make global app revalidation best-effort so cache invalidation failures do not strand restore cleanup or falsely fail committed mutations.
- Add/adjust restore and revalidation tests for acquisition failure, migration failure, and revalidation failure cleanup.

Validation:
- Targeted restore/revalidation tests first.
- Full quality gates before commit.
